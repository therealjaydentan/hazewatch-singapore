Understanding the PSI

A simple guide to common 24-hour PSI bands.

🟢

0–50 · Good

Air quality is generally satisfactory. Continue to follow official updates.

🟡

51–100 · Moderate

Most people can continue normal activities. Sensitive individuals should pay attention to how they feel.

🟠

101–200 · Unhealthy

People who are more sensitive to air pollution may experience symptoms. Consider reducing prolonged or strenuous outdoor activity if advised.

🔴

201–300 · Very unhealthy

Health effects may become more concerning. Follow current official health advice and limit exposure as recommended.

🟣

Above 300 · Hazardous

Serious health effects are possible. Check official advisories promptly and follow instructions.

ℹ️

PSI is not a forecast

Readings describe measured conditions, not what will happen later. Use official forecasts and advisories for decisions.

Protect yourself and others

General reminders, not individual medical advice.

📱

Check official updates

Conditions can change. Check the latest advisories before making decisions about outdoor activities.

🏃

Be mindful outdoors

Consider reducing strenuous activity when air quality worsens, especially if you are sensitive to haze.

❤️

Look out for others

Children, older adults and people with heart or lung conditions may be more sensitive to haze.

🩺

Take symptoms seriously

If you feel unwell, reduce exposure and seek appropriate medical advice. Get urgent help for severe symptoms.
