What can I do?

General guidance — always follow current official advice.

🏃

When planning exerciseCheck current PM2.5 and official health advisories. Reduce or avoid strenuous outdoor activity when air quality is poor.

🏠

Reduce exposureWhen haze is significant, spend more time indoors and keep windows closed if outdoor air is poor.

😷

Need a mask?Follow NEA and MOH guidance. A well-fitted N95 can reduce fine-particle exposure when used correctly; ordinary surgical masks do not filter haze particles as effectively.

💙

Look out for one anotherPeople with heart or lung conditions, older adults, children and pregnant people may be more vulnerable. Seek medical advice if unwell.
